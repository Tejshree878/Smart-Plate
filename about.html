🥗 About Us – Smart Plate

Welcome to Smart Plate – Your Personalized Wellness Companion!

At Smart Plate, we're a team of passionate developers and health enthusiasts who came together with a common goal: to simplify and personalize the journey to a healthier lifestyle through smart technology.

🌟 Our Mission

To empower individuals to take control of their health by combining artificial intelligence with intuitive tools that promote better eating, smarter grocery planning, and personalized fitness routines.

⚙️ What We Offer

  • 🔢BMI Calculator & Health Insights: Calculate your BMI and get categorized with tailored diet tips and supplement suggestions.
  • 🍔Recipe Search with Instructions & Videos: Explore healthy recipes with step-by-step instructions and helpful video guides.
  • 🛒Grocery Management Tool: Keep track of your grocery list easily by adding or removing items in real time.
  • 🚴🏻Fitness Routine Recommender: Get custom exercise plans based on your BMI category and personal health goals.

💡 Why Smart Plate?

We believe that good health shouldn't be complicated. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey or you're a seasoned wellness warrior, Smart Plate is designed to give smart, actionable advice and tools that fit right into your daily routine.

👨‍💻 Built By a Passionate Team
